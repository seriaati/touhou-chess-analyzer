Touhou Chess Analyzer

Set up the position, effects and both hands, then analyze. Uses the game's own rules and engine.

Drag pieces onto the board, and drag them around to move them. Drop a piece outside the board to remove it. Drag effects onto pieces (barriers onto empty squares); click an effect on the board to remove it. Effects on pieces know who cast them from the piece's colour.
